The Tell-Tale Heart

Inspired by the world-renowned tale by Edgar Allan Poe, 1843

It is a little past four in the morning. A neighbour has reported a scream from the old house on the corner. You are a detective, sent with three police officers to search the premises.

The man who answers the door insists that everything is fine. He says the old man who lives here has gone to the country.

In that case, who is the man who answered the door?

Click the outlined door to get started.

How to Play

Find out where the old man is. Search his bedroom one clue at a time. The next clue is always outlined in the room.

Ask the man who answered the door about each clue, then decide whether to believe him or confront him. Believe him too easily, and the case goes unsolved.
